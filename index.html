About Us

Our Coffee Shop is a cozy neighborhood café located at 121 P. Jacinto Street, Grace Park East, South Caloocan, Metro Manila. Known for its warm and welcoming atmosphere, the café provides a comfortable space where customers can enjoy quality coffee, catch up with friends, study, or work in a relaxed setting. With affordable prices and a selection of espresso-based beverages, iced coffee, and light snacks, EDDA Coffee Shop caters to students, professionals, and coffee enthusiasts looking for a pleasant café experience. Its friendly ambiance and convenient location make it a favorite destination for locals seeking a great cup of coffee and a place to unwind.
